### Translate 'good morning' into Spanish and Chinese.
The translation of "good morning" into Spanish is:

¡Buenos días!

And the translation into Chinese is:

早上好! (Wǎng bù duì)

Both translations convey the same meaning in their respective languages. ¡Buenos días! is a common way to greet people in Spanish, while Wǎng bù duì is a more formal way to say "good morning" in Chinese. Both expressions express welcome or greeting for someone who has just arrived at a place of work, school, or other social event.
