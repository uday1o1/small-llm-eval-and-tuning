### What is the capital of France, and why is it famous?
The capital of France is Paris.

Paris is renowned for its historical significance, cultural richness, and iconic landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, Notre-Dame Cathedral, and Montmartre. It has been the seat of government, culture, and religion since ancient times and continues to be an important center of European politics, art, and science.

Paris' fame stems from its rich history, including the influence of various cultures throughout centuries, particularly French, German, Italian, and English influences. The city's architecture, cuisine, and fashion have also made it a popular destination for tourists and locals alike.

### Translate 'good morning' into Spanish and Chinese.
The translation of "good morning" into Spanish is:

¡Buenos días!

And the translation into Chinese is:

早上好! (Wǎng bù duì)

Both translations convey the same meaning in both languages.
