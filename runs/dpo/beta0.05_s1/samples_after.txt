### Translate 'good morning' into Spanish and Chinese.
The translation of "good morning" into Spanish is:

¡Buenos días!

And the translation into Chinese is:

早上好! (Wǎng bù duì)

Both translations convey the same meaning in both languages. ¡Buenos días! is a common way to greet people in Spanish, while "早上好!" is how you would say it in Chinese.
