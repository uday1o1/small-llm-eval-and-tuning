### Translate 'good morning' into Spanish and Chinese.
The translation of "good morning" into Spanish is:

¡Buenos días!

And the translation of "good morning" into Chinese is:

早上好! (Měiyǒu hǎo!)

These translations convey the same meaning in both languages. The first one uses the informal "buenos días" which means "good days" or "morning good times", while the second one uses the more formal "早上好!" which translates to "Good morning!" in English. Both expressions express greeting for someone at their earliest possible time.
