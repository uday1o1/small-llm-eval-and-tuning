### What is the capital of France, and why is it famous?
The capital of France is Paris.

Paris is the most populous city in France and one of the largest cities in Europe. It is known for its rich history, beautiful architecture, and vibrant culture. Here are some reasons why Paris is so famous:

1. Artistic Heritage: Paris has been home to many influential artists throughout history, including Leonardo da Vinci, Michelangelo, and Vincent van Gogh. The city's art scene continues to thrive today with numerous museums, galleries, and cultural institutions.

2. Cuisine: Paris is renowned for its culinary delights, particularly its famous dishes like croissants, baguettes, and pastries.
