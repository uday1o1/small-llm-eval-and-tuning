### What is the capital of France, and why is it famous?
The capital of France is Paris.

Paris is renowned for its rich history, cultural richness, and iconic landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, Notre-Dame Cathedral, and Montmartre. It's also known for its vibrant nightlife, art scene, and fashion industry. The city has been a hub of European culture since ancient times and continues to be an important center of global affairs, politics, and business. Its status as the world's most populous city and its role in shaping modern French identity make it a fascinating place to visit and explore.
