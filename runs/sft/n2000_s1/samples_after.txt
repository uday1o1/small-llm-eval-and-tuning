### Explain what a language model is to a 10-year-old.
A language model is a type of artificial intelligence that is used to predict the next word or phrase in a sentence based on the previous words. It is a type of machine learning algorithm that uses a set of rules to generate predictions. The model is trained on a large amount of data, such as text or speech, and it uses this data to learn how to make predictions. The model is then used to generate new sentences or words based on the input data.

### What is the capital of France, and why is it famous?
