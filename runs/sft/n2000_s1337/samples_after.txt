### Explain what a language model is to a 10-year-old.
A language model is a type of artificial intelligence that is used to predict the next word or phrase that a person or computer will say or write. It is a type of machine learning algorithm that uses statistical methods to analyze large amounts of data and make predictions about future words or phrases. Language models are used in a variety of applications, including natural language processing, speech recognition, and machine translation.
