### Explain what a language model is to a 10-year-old.
A language model is a type of artificial intelligence (AI) that is used to predict the next word or phrase that a person or computer will type or say. It is a type of machine learning algorithm that is used to analyze and understand human language.

Language models are trained on a large dataset of text, such as books, articles, or social media posts. They are then used to identify patterns and predict the next word or phrase that a person or computer will type or say.

### What is the capital of France, and why is it famous?
The capital of France is Paris, which is located in the region of Normandy in the north-east of the country. It is also known as the City of Light, due to its rich history and cultural significance. Paris is famous for its iconic landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre Museum, and the Notre Dame Cathedral. It is also home to many famous artists, writers, and musicians, including Pablo Picasso, Vincent van Gogh, and Claude Monet.
